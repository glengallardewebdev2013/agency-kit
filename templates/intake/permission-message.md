# Permission message

Send this before collecting anything. Their written "yes" (a reply in
Messenger, email or the intake form checkbox) is the permission record; save a
screenshot of it to `intake/raw/client-uploads/permission.png`.

---

Hi [Name]! To get your free website preview ready fast, we'd like to use
what's already on your pages:

- Facebook: [link]
- Instagram: [link]
- [other links]

We'll use your logo, your photos and videos, and your business details
(services, hours, contact info) to build the first draft. We won't use photos
of customers or patients unless you tell us they've agreed, and we'll leave
out anything you ask us to.

Could you reply "Yes, go ahead" so we have your OK in writing?

Quicker for both of us: if you're an admin of the page, you can send us a
copy of your photos in one go. On Facebook: Settings > Download your
information > pick your Page > Photos and videos. Or just drop your favourite
photos and your logo into this chat or a Google Drive folder.

Thank you!
