---
title: Five signs your water heater needs attention
summary: Rumbling, rusty water and slow recovery are early warnings. Here's what each one means.
published: 2026-10-01
---
Most water heaters give warning signs before they fail. Catching them early can save you a flooded basement.

## 1. Rumbling or popping

Sediment builds up at the bottom of the tank. Flushing it once a year helps.

## 2. Rusty water

Rust-coloured hot water can mean the tank is corroding from the inside.
