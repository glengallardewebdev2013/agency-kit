Every GallarDesign site starts with a designer, not a template. We look at your business, your customers and what you need the site to do, whether that's bookings, quote requests or calls, and design the pages around it.

Then we build it on a modern, fast setup. Pages load in a blink, work on every phone and meet accessibility basics, so more visitors stay and more of them get in touch. Search titles, descriptions and the technical basics Google looks for are in place from launch.

## What's included

- Up to 5, 12 or more pages, depending on your plan
- A design made for your business
- Hosting, SSL and a contact, booking or quote form
- Search and tracking basics set up before launch
- Edits every month, done within two business days
