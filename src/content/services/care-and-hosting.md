**Care Plus, ₱1,990 a month:** everything in Basic Care, plus 1 hour of edits a month, done within 1 working day when you send a Messenger or Viber message, and a one-page monthly report.

**Growth Care, ₱4,990 a month:** everything in Care Plus, plus 2 blog posts a month, Google Business Profile posts and review replies, and local SEO tracking.

You can also post to your blog and change your search titles yourself in a simple editor, on any plan.
