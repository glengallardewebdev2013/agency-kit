Every plan includes hosting, SSL, backups and edits for the first 12 months. After that, care keeps it all running.

Send any change, from a new photo to a new page, and it's done within two business days. You can also post to your blog and update your search titles yourself in a simple editor.

Care is $79 a month for Starter sites and $129 a month for Business sites. If you'd rather host the site yourself, it's yours to take.
